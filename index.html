Northsignal Studio

A personal tool that produces and publishes videos to its owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Writes, renders and schedules short and long-form videos on the owner's own computer.
  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.
  • Reads back view counts and analytics for those same channels, to decide what to make next.

Who can use it

Only its owner. The tool is not offered to other people, has no accounts, no sign-up and no users other than the owner of the Google account it is authorised with.

Google data it uses

  • Upload videos — to publish to the owner's channels.
  • Read the YouTube account — to confirm the correct channel is connected before uploading.
  • Read YouTube Analytics — to record how the owner's own videos perform.

Nothing is read from, or written to, any other person's account.
